# Chooser of the Slain

A Papers, Please-style judgment game: you're a new Valkyrie with 20 battle-days until Ragnarök, sorting the dead before dusk. It's planned as a paid release on Steam and Google Play, with a free web demo (the Daily Shift and campaign days 1–3).

- **Plan:** [`docs/build-plan.md`](docs/build-plan.md): design, 20-day campaign, milestones, risks.
- **Technical spec:** [`docs/tech-spec.md`](docs/tech-spec.md): types, the case-fairness system, content formats, platform shells.
- **Roadmap:** [`docs/roadmap.md`](docs/roadmap.md): what's queued, what's blocked, and what waits on you.
- **Status:** M7 (engineering done): the 20-day campaign is playable in the full builds, with a first draft of the story for every day and eleven endings (nine new, plus demoted and an empty house), all reachable by bots; winter bills, late-game upgrades and Endless mode ([`docs/m7-design.md`](docs/m7-design.md)). Waiting on you: rewriting or signing off the story drafts ([`docs/story-drafts.md`](docs/story-drafts.md)) and outside playthroughs, which the economy's tuning needs. and a god at the desk: on Day 18 Odin stops at the desk between souls, with the sun held while he talks; and a jarl's bribe: on Day 9 a jarl who died in his bed walks past the whole line and offers rings for a Valhalla stamp, said openly on the desk, and taking them is a mistake all the same, paid at the audit and remembered; and family trouble money can't fix: a scene can cost the next day's sun (a trip home at dawn), and on Night 10 your mother needs carrying up the hill that mends any woman who climbs it, or else; Ulf may go up to the pass with the jarl's levy, and on Day 16 a neighbour's son comes to the desk from it and asks, openly, to be sent to his drowned mother; and the Norns' weave: once a run has reached an ending, a new run can be woven, reading the same rules in another order (Rán's before the registry's and the clerk's, or the clerk's after every hall's), with two souls a day that the new order decides; and a Ragnarök you fight: after the last night the hosts the run filled go to four fronts (the wolf, the fire, Hel's gate, the shore) in the order the player sets, the screen saying as it's set which will hold; souls sent to the wrong hall break and run there, named on the horn, the battle and the ending's report, and the endings that tell of the battle need their fronts held; and the endings now tell what became of everyone: fuller ending texts, and an epilogue of the family, the powers and the story's own souls, read from how the run left them; and rings with a job late in the run, and a softer bottom: from Night 13 the quartermaster sells arms for the last battle, a lot a night for the front you choose, each dearer than the last; upgrades sell back for half their price; and once a run Skögul pays the debt that would have cost you your wings; and Day 4 now teaches Freyja's stamp alone, with requests and day events from Day 5 and the first promotion from Day 6; Loki has a favour of his own, a ring a nail for every soul sent on with its nails uncut; and the clerk keeps his contract a day longer for one who said "Not yet" and stayed in his favour; and a harder last battle with a second choice: the foes are a fifth stronger, and at the horn you choose which front you ride to yourself; and the one-off systems used more often: Freyja, Loki in a shawl and Hel come to the desk (Days 11, 15, 17), a miser pays for a stamp home and walks (Day 13), Odin changes his claim at noon (Day 18), ordinary souls plead for another hall on about half the days, standing there at Ragnarök if granted, and there are two more day events and a third weave; and souls who come back: the husband, wife or cousin of a soul sent where it didn't belong comes to the desk later, says so, and asks to join it, and five more story souls come because of how earlier ones were stamped (a woman sent home who comes back dead, Geir's widow, the miser's nephew, the jarl's son, Thorvald's mother), 25 story cases in all; ## Getting started

Requires Node 22.18+ and pnpm 10.

```sh
pnpm install
pnpm dev                 # dev-full build with the Case Lab, at http://localhost:5173
pnpm lint                # Biome + import boundaries + engine purity
pnpm typecheck           # engine (no DOM), Node tooling, browser code
pnpm test                # Vitest + fast-check
pnpm build:all           # all seven targets into dist/<target>
pnpm leak-check          # demo builds contain no campaign content; full builds do
pnpm e2e                 # Playwright against dist/web-demo, dist/dev-full and dist/web-playtest
pnpm sim sweep --seeds 200   # generator sweep with the CI thresholds (nightly runs 10,000)
pnpm sim sweep --daily --seeds 200   # the same for Dailies #1-#200
pnpm sim compare --set 'days[*].economy.wage+=1'   # the same bots and seeds with a number changed, and what moved (tech-spec §63)
pnpm playtest:read reports/   # sum up playtest reports (issue bodies saved as files) beside the bots (docs/playtest.md)
pnpm playtest:keep backup.json --name tester   # keep a tester's saves as tests that every change must open
pnpm golden:update       # refresh tests/golden (day summaries, Daily checksums) after an intended generator change
pnpm store:capture       # store screenshots, GIFs and trailer frames into dist/store (pnpm build:electron-full first)
pnpm build:steam         # the Steam build: electron-full and its Electron shell (docs/steam.md)
pnpm steam               # run it (once first: node apps/electron/node_modules/electron/install.js)
pnpm e2e:steam           # the shell under Electron (on Linux: xvfb-run -a pnpm e2e:steam)
pnpm package:steam       # the folder Steam ships, in dist/steam/full
```

## Layout

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `packages/engine` | Deterministic game engine: pure TypeScript, no DOM, integer math only |
| `packages/content-schema` | zod schemas and the build-target matrix |
| `packages/content-compiler` | Compiles `content/packs` into per-target bundles and leak tokens |
| `packages/ui` | Preact UI (desk layout for desktop/Deck, drawer layout for phones) |
| `packages/platform` | Web, itch, Electron and Android adapters, chosen at build time |
| `packages/art` | Body art behind a swappable provider contract: the woodcut (the chosen direction and the default), plus the pixel candidate and the placeholder for comparison |
| `apps/web` | The single Vite entry for every target |
| `apps/electron` | The Steam shell (Electron): the game's window, saves as files, Steam's achievements ([`docs/steam.md`](docs/steam.md)) |
| `apps/android` | The Google Play shell (M9) |
| `apps/telemetry` | Opt-in alpha telemetry: a Cloudflare Worker and D1 schema |
| `content/packs` | `core`, `daily` (the Daily Shift spec), `demo` (days 1–3) and `campaign` (days 4–20) |
| `assets` | Sound files by pack (`assets/<pack>/sound/`), none yet: see [`docs/sound-brief.md`](docs/sound-brief.md) |
| `tools` | Leak check, boundary lint, generator sweeps, icon renderer |
